#what i learned and understood
##repositories
A repository is where your project's hisory is kept.
##cloning
cloning is used to add or remove files,easier to fix issues.
##commiting
commiting saves the changes into the project history

##branches
Enables one to isolate work that you don't want to merge with the final project
##forks
usually used when you want to contribute to someone else's project
##pull request
Here you tell others about the changes you want to make and ask for their feedback(maybe what they would love me to change)
##issues
keeps track of all tasks you want to work on for your project and let others know what you plan to work on.
##user profile
lets future employers and other people know more about you,your work and the contributiond you have made so far.
##markdown
Helps organize information and make it easier for others to read.
##following users
you are able to follow people so as to receive notifications about their acivities and discover projects in their communities.
